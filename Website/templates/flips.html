How to Use My Flips

Basics

My Flips is your working list of open and completed Grand Exchange flips, with totals that update as you add buys, sells, and historical views.

Adding flips

Use New Flip to record a buy, sell, or adjustment. Once saved, the item appears in the table and contributes to the summary cards at the top.

Deleting flips

Open the ellipsis menu and choose Delete Flip. Then select the item you want to remove and confirm the delete prompt.

Opening item details

Select an item row to review the individual flips that make up that item position.

Sorting the table

Click column headers to sort the table by that column. Click again to switch the sort direction.

Filtering

Filters help you narrow the table to the flips that matter right now, without losing the rest of your journal or portfolio data.

Combining filters

You can apply multiple filters at the same time, such as completion state, position size, quantity, realized profit, unrealized profit, or time held.

Searching within results

Use the search box to narrow visible rows by item name while your active filters remain applied.

Sorting by applied filters

When filters are active, the sorted-by indicator lets you sort using filter-related fields so you can rank the results you narrowed down.

Clearing filters

Active filter tags show what is currently affecting the table. Clear individual filters when you want to widen the view again.

Charting

The Equity Chart gives you a read-only picture of how your flip portfolio has moved over time.

Net profit and loss

The graph displays your net profit and loss from the date range you select, using the available price history for your flips.

Date ranges

Choose a range like 30D, 90D, or 1Y to rebuild the chart around that period.

Journal

Each item has its own journal so you can keep the reasoning behind a trade next to the numbers that prove or disprove it.

What a journal can hold

  • Entry reason and trade strategy
  • Confidence score and tags
  • Time stamped notes as the trade develops
  • Exit reasons linked to specific sells

A useful workflow

When you open a trade, jot down your general thesis: why the item, why now, and what would make you exit. As new information becomes available, add time stamped notes to update that thesis instead of rewriting the original thought.

Exit context

When you sell, use exit reasons to connect the decision back to the specific sell. Over time, that makes it easier to see which strategies are actually working.

Reviewing patterns

Tags and confidence scores make it easier to compare similar trades later, especially when you want to review what worked across multiple items.

historical

Historical view lets you inspect what your portfolio would have looked like at a specific moment in the past.

Selecting a date

Choose a date or quick preset. The website calculates your portfolio standing at that date using the available historical item data.

Adjusted totals

The summary cards at the top adjust to reflect your entire holdings as they looked at that moment.

Adjusted table

The table also updates, so item rows and values reflect the historical snapshot instead of current prices.

Returning to now

Use the historical banner controls to return to the real-time view when you are done reviewing the snapshot.
