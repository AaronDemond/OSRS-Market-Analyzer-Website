Understanding Live Feedback

Live Feedback Watches Real-time Price Watch

Live Feedback tracks whether your chosen buy or sell price is still competitive against the latest market price. It refreshes automatically so you can spot when you have been beaten and need to adjust.

How It Works

Choose an item, pick whether you are watching the buy side or sell side, and enter your target price. Live Feedback compares your price to the current competing market price and labels the watch so you can see whether your offer is still safe.

  • Buy watch: Checks whether someone is offering a higher buy price than you.
  • Sell watch: Checks whether someone is offering a lower sell price than you.
  • Gap: Shows the GP difference between your price and the competing market price.

Fields And Statuses

  • Item: Search and select the exact item you want to monitor.
  • Price: Enter the GP price you currently want to hold in the market.
  • Email or SMS: Choose how you want to be notified when a watch becomes triggered.
  • Safe: Your price is still competitive.
  • Undercut or Overcut: Another player has beaten your price.
  • No price: There is not enough current market data to compare.

Example Scenario

You place a buy offer for an item at 1,250,000 gp. If another buyer moves above you, the watch flips to triggered so you know your offer is no longer leading. You can then edit the watch or adjust your offer in game.
